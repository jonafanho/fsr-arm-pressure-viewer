FSR Signal Waveform

F0–F7 (V)
8 FSR channels are sampled by the ESP32-WROOM-32E ADC and sent over USB serial at 921600 baud. FSR voltage = ADC / 4095 × 3.3 V. GPIO 32/33/34/35 are ADC1; GPIO 25/26/27/13 are ADC2. This firmware does not use Wi-Fi, so ADC2 is available. Recording and ZIP export are handled by the local Python service.
